Stats & Profiles

All statistics come from live sources — nothing is invented. C&C Online (with Shatabrick as its ladder site) and RA3BattleNet are labelled separately, while the online total combines whichever platforms the server has enabled.

The stats panel

/stats opens an interactive view for you (the reply is private); /stats_panel set <channel> puts a persistent panel in a channel. The panel refreshes every 10 minutes and carries the classic charts below it, one chart per message:

  • Online Players (Last 24 Hours) — from tracked snapshots
  • New Players (Last 30 Days) — RA3 players appearing on the ladders for the first time
  • Online Players (Last 30 Days) — daily peaks from tracked history

Generals Evolution servers can use C&C Online and RA3BattleNet. Its online count includes only players identified in Generals Evolution lobbies by map or platform mod metadata. Idle platform users cannot be assigned to a mod. RA3BattleNet seasons, factions and Masters remain exclusive to Red Alert 3.

Player profiles

/profile works two ways:

  • /profile user:<member> — the linked accounts of a Discord member (manage both platforms with /link).
  • /profile player:<name or id> — any player on Shatabrick or RA3BattleNet. A Discord link is not required.

Shatabrick and RA3BattleNet stay separate in the profile. Shatabrick's public profile pages provide its rank image, level, score and W/L records for Unranked, Ranked 1v1/2v2 and Clan 1v1/2v2.

On Red Alert 3 servers, the RA3BattleNet section shows, per mode:
