Stats & Profiles

All statistics come from live sources — nothing is invented. The two platforms are shown side by side and never mixed: C&C Online (with Shatabrick as its ladder site) and RA3BattleNet.

The stats panel

/stats opens an interactive view for you (the reply is private); /stats_panel set <channel> puts a persistent panel in a channel. The panel refreshes every 10 minutes and carries the classic charts below it, one chart per message:

  • Online Players (Last 24 Hours) — from tracked snapshots
  • New Players (Last 30 Days) — players appearing on the ladders for the first time
  • Online Players (Last 30 Days) — daily peaks from tracked history

Player profiles

/profile works two ways:

  • /profile user:<member> — the linked accounts of a Discord member (link yours with /link_ra3battlenet and /link_shatabrick).
  • /profile player:<name or id> — any player on the RA3BattleNet ladders, Discord account or not.

The RA3BattleNet section shows, per mode:

1v1: 1234 elo · #12 · 15W/4L (79%) · 🇺🇸
2v2: Placement (4 left) · 1W/1L · 🇷🇺
Seasons: S9 Amphoreus 1v1: 963 (#111) · S9 2v2: 965 (#322)
Not on this season's ladders? Link (or look up) with your numeric persona id — the number in your ra3battle.cn profile URL.
