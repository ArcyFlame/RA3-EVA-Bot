Tournaments

E.V.A runs a game-isolated tournament pipeline. RA3 uses the GameReplays.org esports portal and events forum; Generals Evolution uses official ModDB announcements. New tournaments, sign-up threads, brackets and results show up in your channels without anyone doing anything.

How discovery works

  1. Portal scan — new tournament announcements are stored and posted to your tournament channel with a Sign Up button.
  2. Forum scan — the bot pairs forum topics with announcements by name: "FTW 91 Registration" (sign-up thread) and "FTW 91 Brackets, Results and Replays" (results thread). Edition numbers are respected — FTW 91 never pairs with FTW 90.
  3. Challonge links are extracted from results threads. A tournament can run several brackets (qualifiers per server, group stage, playoffs) — all are tracked, the playoff bracket is the primary one.
  4. Facts — prize pool, format, map pool and the player list are extracted from the threads (prize tables, donations, team rosters) and from Challonge as a fallback.

What you can browse

/events

An interactive browser: Date, Status, Format, Prize, Map Pool, with Sign Up / Results buttons and Previous / Refresh / Next navigation.

Administrators, moderators and referees also see a private Edit Details button. It can correct a missing date, status, prize, format or map pool for either Red Alert 3 or Generals Evolution. Staff corrections are kept when the automatic scanners run again.

/results

Podium and numbered standings from each event's Challonge brackets — one entry per bracket for multi-bracket events (e.g. Gathering Storm's CCO qualifier, RA3BattleNet qualifier and playoffs).

/matches

The current bracket: header with players/format/state, the same podium and standings view once it finishes, upcoming matches while it runs.

/tournaments_scan

Admins can scan the official sources for their server's selected game instead of waiting for the next automatic check.

Check-ins & referees

Run /checkin [event] to open the current tournament board. Referees can turn personal DM alerts on or off for new registrations and check-ins. Players see numbered registered, checked-in and missing lists. Referees get the management menu for opening the board, refreshing registrations and posting it to the tournament channel. The referee role is configured in /bot_setup.

Score reports

/report_score <opponent> <factions> <score> uses the checked-in player list for opponent suggestions. Reports go to a referee for approval and can update the current Challonge match when it is an exact match for the two players.

Winners & Hall of Fame

Champions are recorded from Challonge rankings, final-match results and explicitly labelled forum finals for older events without a bracket. A resumable scan covers the full GameReplays esports forum history. Wins feed the Tournament Wins (Top 10) stats page above the community-maintained Hall of Fame (/add_master).
