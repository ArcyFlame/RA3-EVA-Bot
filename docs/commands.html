Activity XP and replay ratings

Command What it does
/activity leaderboard Privately view the top 10 activity members.
/activity admin Configure XP, days, levels and cosmetic rank roles. Admin only.
/activity xp <member> <amount> Add XP or use a negative amount to remove XP. Admin/moderator only.
/activity role <member> <action> [role] Add a configured cosmetic rank role, remove rank roles or restore automatic XP ranking. Admin/moderator only.

Rank details now live in /profile. Pings count once per UTC day; ordinary chat earns no XP.

Generals Evolution replay cards

In /activity admin, select the replay channel under XP Sources, then open Replay Ratings. Each accepted replay file gets a separate card with thumbs-up and thumbs-down reactions. Original uploads are preserved.

The default upload award is 25 XP, up to three files per member per UTC day. Rating bonuses start at two net positive votes, with 5 XP per net vote, up to 100 XP per replay and 100 bonus XP per uploader per UTC day. Voter accounts must be seven days old. These values are configurable.

Self-votes, bot votes, duplicate files and repeatedly removing/re-adding reactions do not earn extra XP. Downvotes reduce future bonuses, not earned XP. These limits reduce abuse but cannot prevent collusion between real members.

Scan Recent Uploads checks the last 100 messages from the past 30 days, inspects at most 20 files and creates at most 10 missing cards per run. Scans have a 10-minute cooldown. Historical uploads do not get backdated upload XP.

The bot needs View Channel, Send Messages, Embed Links, Read Message History and Add Reactions. Staff role overrides stay pinned until automatic ranking is restored.
