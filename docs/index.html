Introduction

E.V.A is a Discord bot built for the Command & Conquer: Red Alert 3 community. Named after the Allied command voice, it keeps RA3 servers alive with live player statistics, automatic tournament coverage, news feeds and community tools — everything in one place across GameReplays, C&C Online, Shatabrick and RA3BattleNet.

📊 Live Stats

Online players from C&C Online and RA3BattleNet, leaderboards, map stats, faction popularity and classic Red Alert-styled charts on a persistent channel panel.

🏆 Tournaments

Automatic scanning of the GameReplays esports portal and forum: announcements, sign-ups, prizes, map pools and Challonge brackets — including qualifiers and playoffs.

🏅 Player Profiles

Link Shatabrick and RA3BattleNet from one manager. Profiles keep both platforms separate and show the live rankings each source provides.

📰 News & Feeds

GameReplays RA3 news, ModDB mods and articles, Twitch streams and YouTube uploads — posted to your channels, deduplicated, rate-limited.

🛠️ Community Tools

Verified tournament map picking, clans, tips, moderation utilities and an admin setup wizard.

🎮 Multi-Game

Each server picks its game — Red Alert 3, Kane's Wrath or Generals Evolution — and the bot adapts its feeds and stats sections.

Quick start

Add the bot to your server, run /bot_setup to choose your admin role, channels and features, and you are done. The full walkthrough lives in Setup; every command is listed in Commands.
